How do you turn a standard short-term rental stay into an unforgettable local experience? 

🏡✨We have been building our short-term rental unit, and so I’ve been quiet on LinkedIn. As we welcome our first guests this weekend, I was thinking that generic travel brochures just don't cut it. Guests want genuine local context: the best morning coffee, hidden waterfront walking trails, secret fishing spots, and heritage landmarks—all measured by real travel time from the front door.

To elevate the guest experience, I built a dedicated **Interactive Map Engine** combined with an AI concierge:

🗺️ **Custom Hub & Radius Engine**: Dynamically showcases curated points of interest around the hub with interactive distance rings (actually drive time polygons), category filtering, and instant navigation using a custom Valhalla-based isochrone creator. 

⚡ **Serverless GCP Architecture**: Hosted directly on Google Cloud Storage for the interactive map using MapLibre, displaying terrain (as 100m can be a 2 min stroll or a 10 min slog around here!).

🤖 **Conversational AI Co-Host**: Integrated with a **Google NotebookLM Welcome Manual** that transforms our house guide, appliance walk-throughs, and insider recommendations into an instant Q&A assistant for guests.

Why leave guests with a dusty binder when you can give them a custom geospatial tool and an AI assistant right on their phones? (We will make the binder as well, as sometimes paper beats tech!)
